✅ Bulk Rules
  • Beat last week: +1 rep or +2.5kg on every lift. Grey numbers in the log show last week
  • Hit the week's RIR target. Isolation last sets can go to 0
  • Weigh daily, judge on the weekly average
  • Under +0.25kg/week for 2 weeks → add 150 kcal (≈40g carbs)
  • Over +0.5kg/week after week 2 → drop 150 kcal
  • Waist up more than 1cm per kg gained → drop 150 kcal

  • Sleep 7.5-9 hours. Growth happens here, not in the gym
  • Log every working set — if not written, it didn't happen
